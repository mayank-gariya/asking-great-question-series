# Asking Great Questions Series

## Vision

This series explores the **art and science of asking impactful questions** — the foundational skill that separates problem-solvers from critical thinkers.

Great questions drive innovation, reveal assumptions, and unlock better solutions. This repo demonstrates how strategic questioning can reshape technical and business challenges.

## What's Inside

Jupyter notebooks exploring:
- **How** do we frame problems differently?
- **Why** do conventional approaches fail?
- **What if** we questioned our assumptions?
- **Where** do breakthrough insights come from?

## Why This Matters

In a world of infinite data and solutions, **the differentiator is asking the right questions first**. This repo showcases my approach to thinking critically and differently about real-world problems.

---

*Because better questions lead to better answers.*
